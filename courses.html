For beginners & aspiring professionals

3-Month Professional Bartending

3 Months · Weekday & weekend batches Regular fee ₹35,000 Now ₹25,000/-

The complete foundation, from basic bar mechanics to running a busy service. 5 parts and 30+ modules covering tools, spirits science and stock control.

Ask About This Course: 3-Month Professional Bartending
  1. Part 1

    Fundamentals of Professional Bartending

    Introduction to bartending, history & evolution, bartender vs mixologist vs flair bartender, 19 commercial bar types, hygiene, sanitation, personal safety & professional grooming etiquette.

  2. Part 2

    Bar Tools, Glassware & Service

    Shakers, strainers, jiggers, spoons & equipment, glassware classification & chilling, speed pouring & measurement accuracy, ice science, garnishing, knife skills & guest handling.

  3. Part 3

    Beverage Fundamentals

    Fermentation chemistry, distillation processes (pot vs column stills), classification of alcoholic beverages, liqueur production (maceration & infusion), and mixers & soft drinks.

  4. Part 4

    Comprehensive Spirits & Beverages Encyclopedia

    Origin, production & tasting: Whisky, Vodka, Gin, Rum, Tequila & Mezcal, Brandy & Cognac, Beer (ales & lagers), Wine & sparkling, Cider, Bitters, Absinthe, Sake & Mead.

  5. Part 5

    Bottle-Level Measurement & Spirit Stock Control

    Measurement techniques, portion control, preventing wastage, pilferage control, inventory pars, variance auditing, cost control & daily sales reporting.

For working bartenders & aspiring mixologists

6-Month Advanced Mixology

6 Months · Weekday & weekend batches Regular fee ₹60,000 Now ₹45,000/-

Elite beverage architecture and modern cocktail craft. 8 advanced modules: molecular mixology, house-made ingredients, field visits, bar management and personal branding.

Ask About This Course: 6-Month Advanced Mixology
  1. Module 1

    Advanced Molecular Mixology

    Gelification, basic & reverse spherification, molecular caviar, foams & emulsions, flavour encapsulation, smoke & bubble cocktails, dry ice safety, smoking gun & dehydrated garnishes.

  2. Module 2

    Advanced Cocktail Development

    Flavour profiling, balancing, sensory evaluation (appearance, aroma, finish), texture & mouthfeel, and signature cocktail prototyping from concept to final serve.

  3. Module 3

    Homemade Mixology Ingredients

    Artisanal syrups (simple, rich, oleo saccharum), shrubs, handcrafted bitters, spirit infusions, cordials, reductions, food safety & shelf-life tracking.

  4. Module 4

    Advanced Cocktail Techniques

    Milk & agar clarification, fat washing & freeze-separation, forced carbonation, density layering, flambé & smoking, and hand-carved clear ice.

  5. Module 5

    Industry Field Immersion

    Commercial distillery visits, craft brewery tours, vineyard & winery immersion, meadery fermentation, cocktail bar station shadowing and reflective journals.

  6. Module 6

    Bar Management & Costing

    Costing per serve, recipe costing, menu pricing & margins, inventory, par levels, stock rotation, opening/closing SOPs & Maharashtra licensing compliance.

  7. Module 7

    Social Media & Career Development

    Content creation, personal branding, brand collaborations, networking, careers in luxury hotels, lounges, cruise lines & international markets, CV & interview prep.

  8. Module 8

    Final Practical Capstone

    An original signature cocktail, a molecular showpiece serve, a costing sheet, a live speed and hygiene assessment, and a presentation to a professional jury.
