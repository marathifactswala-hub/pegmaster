Our Story

More Than a Skill

Peg Master Bartending Academy was founded in Pune by Peg Master Avdhut to teach bartending the way it’s done behind a real, busy bar.

Why We Exist

Built at the Counter

Millions watch Avdhut talk spirits online. The academy is where he teaches it in person.

Most people learn bartending from videos or on the job, one mistake at a time. We built the academy so you can learn it properly: the science of what’s in the bottle, the technique behind every pour, and the habits that keep a bar running and profitable.

Classes stay small, practice happens at a real bar counter with real tools, and every student who completes a course gets an official Peg Master certificate and career guidance.

Written on Our Wall

Learn · Practice · Master

The 4 words on our reception mural are how every course is built.

  1. 01

    Learn

    Spirits, fermentation, distillation and flavour, explained properly in the classroom.

  2. 02

    Practice

    Hours behind the practice bar until speed and precision become muscle memory.

  3. 03

    Master

    Advanced techniques, molecular serves and your own signature cocktail.

  4. 04

    Build Your Career

    CV, interviews, personal branding and industry contacts for your first role.
