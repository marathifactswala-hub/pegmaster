View 8-Module Syllabus Breakdown

Detailed syllabus from the official Peg Master 6-Month Advanced Mixology Course:

Module 1 Advanced Molecular Mixology

Gelification, basic & reverse spherification, molecular caviar, foams & emulsions, flavour encapsulation, smoke & bubble cocktails, dry ice safety protocols, smoking gun & dehydrated molecular garnishes.

Module 2 Advanced Cocktail Development

Flavour profiling & primary wheels, incremental balancing, sensory evaluation (appearance, aroma, finish), texture & mouthfeel engineering, and signature cocktail prototyping from concept to final serve.

Module 3 Homemade Mixology Ingredients

Artisanal syrups (simple, rich, oleo saccharum), fruit-and-vinegar shrubs, custom handcrafted bitters, spirit infusions, bespoke cordials, reductions, food safety, refrigeration & shelf-life tracking.

Module 4 Advanced Cocktail Techniques

Milk & agar clarification, fat washing & freeze-separation, forced carbonation, density layering, flambé & smoking, and hand-carved directional clear ice techniques.

Module 5 Industry Field Immersion & Practical Learning

Commercial distillery visits, craft brewery tours, vineyard & winery immersion, meadery fermentation observation, premium cocktail bar station shadowing, and student reflective review journals.

Module 6 Bar Management & Costing — Basics to Pro

Beverage costing per serve, average recipe costing, menu pricing formulas & profit margins, inventory management, par levels, stock rotation, opening/closing SOPs & Maharashtra licensing compliance.

Module 7 Social Media & Career Development

Bartender content creation, personal branding, brand collaborations, industry networking, career opportunities in luxury hotels, cocktail lounges, cruise lines & international markets, CV & interview prep.

Module 8 Final Practical Capstone Project

Creation of an original signature cocktail, molecular showpiece serve, commercial costing sheet, live speed and hygiene assessment, and final presentation to professional jury.

No. 07 — FAQ

Questions at the Bar

Do I need any prior experience?

No. The 3-Month Professional Bartending course starts from the ground up, covering fundamental bar tools, full spirits theory and hands-on speed pouring. If you are a working bartender or want to master molecular mixology, custom ingredient creation and bar management, the 6-Month Advanced Mixology course will elevate your craft to elite global standards.

What is the difference between the 3-Month and 6-Month courses?

The 3-Month Professional Bartending course builds the complete core foundation: bar mechanics, tools, full spirits knowledge (12+ categories), and bottle-level stock control. The 6-Month Advanced Mixology course is an advanced master program covering molecular mixology (spherification, foams, smoke, dry ice), clarification & fat washing, in-house syrups & bitters, field immersion visits to distilleries & breweries, bar costing, personal branding, and a signature cocktail capstone project.

What are the fees and batch timings?

Fees depend on the course and batch. Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll send the latest fees, dates and timings.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Every student who completes a course receives an official Peg Master Bartending Academy certificate.

Do you help with jobs after the course?

We guide you with interview preparation, CV tips, personal branding, and industry contacts across hotels, bars, cruise lines and event companies.

Is there an age requirement?

Students must meet the legal age requirement for handling and tasting alcohol under Maharashtra law. Contact us if you’re unsure.
