Curriculum

What You’ll Learn

Skills that hotels, bars, cruise lines and event companies actually hire for, taught hands-on.

  • Spirits Knowledge

    Whisky, rum, vodka, gin, tequila and liqueurs: how they’re made and how to serve them.

  • Cocktail Craft

    Classics, modern twists and balancing sweet, sour, bitter and strong.

  • Flair Bartending

    Bottle and tin tricks, working flair and showstopping fire flair.

  • Garnish & Presentation

    Garnishes, glassware and plating that make every drink camera-ready.

  • Bar Operations

    Setup, hygiene, inventory, costing and running a busy service.

  • Guest Experience

    Reading guests, responsible serving and upselling with confidence.

FAQ

Questions, Answered

Do I need any prior experience?

No. The Professional Bartending course starts from the basics. If you already work behind a bar, the Advanced Mixology & Flair course will push your skills further.

What are the fees and batch timings?

Fees depend on the course and batch. Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll send the latest fees, dates and timings.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Every student who completes a course receives a Peg Master Bartending Academy certificate.

Do you help with jobs after the course?

We guide you with interview preparation, CV tips and industry contacts to help you start your bartending career.

Is there an age requirement?

Students must meet the legal age requirement for handling and tasting alcohol under Maharashtra law. Contact us if you’re unsure.
