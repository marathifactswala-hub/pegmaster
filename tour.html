Academy Tour · Pune

Step Inside

Walk through the academy the way you would on your first day: from the front door to the practice bar.

8 Stops

The Walk-Through

Every room has a job in your training.

  1. 01

    The Entrance

    Find the gold Peg Master wall with the Pune skyline beneath it. The glass door beside it is your way in.

  2. 02

    The Welcome Lobby

    Step through the welcome door into the lobby. You can wait on the benches here before your counselling chat or your first class.

  3. 03

    Reception & the Flair Wall

    The big flair mural sums up the academy: learn, practise, master and build your career. Visitors sign in at the desk.

  4. 04

    The Counselling Room

    Sit down with the team to talk through the two courses, fees, batch timings and where you want your career to go.

  5. 05

    The Practice Bar

    A real wooden counter with a stocked back bar. This is where you build speed, precision and confidence with real tools.

  6. 06

    The Theory Classroom

    Spirits science, the 12+ spirit categories, costing and stock control are taught here before you take them to the bar.

  7. 07

    The Training Floor

    An open matted floor with room to move, for technique drills, group sessions and flair practice.

  8. 08

    The Content Studio

    A Peg Master logo backdrop for photos and reels. Your personal-branding module comes to life here.
